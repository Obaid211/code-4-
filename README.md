🛰️ SmartAgri: Space-to-Field IntelligenceSmartAgri is an advanced precision farming platform that integrates local sensor data with global satellite intelligence to optimize crop yield and resource management.🌟 Key Satellite FeaturesMulti-Spectral Crop Health Analysis: Utilizing Sentinel-2 satellite data to calculate the NDVI (Normalized Difference Vegetation Index). This allows farmers to monitor crop vigor and identify pest infestations or nutrient deficiencies 10–14 days before they are visible to the naked eye.Precision Thermal Mapping: Integration of Landsat-9 Thermal Infrared (TIRS) sensors to map Land Surface Temperature (LST). This identifies "Heat Stress" zones in the field, allowing for targeted irrigation and preventing crop wilting.Automated Acreage Calculation: A specialized Geospatial AI engine that automatically calculates the total hectares of healthy vegetation within a defined boundary, providing accurate seasonal yield forecasts.Interactive Dual-Pane Visualization: A "Swipe-Map" interface that allows users to compare raw satellite true-color imagery with mathematical vegetation indices in real-time.🛠️ Technical ArchitectureRemote Sensing Engine: Google Earth Engine (GEE) API.Data Processing: Python (earthengine-api, geemap).Satellite Constellations: * Sentinel-2: 10m resolution multi-spectral imagery.Landsat-9: 30m resolution thermal and panchromatic data.Analysis Algorithms: * NDVI: $\frac{NIR - Red}{NIR + Red}$ for biomass detection.LST Calibration: Radiometric thermal conversion for surface temperature monitoring.Web Framework: Flask/Django (integrated with Folium for interactive GIS rendering).
